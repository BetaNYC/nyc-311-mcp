Ask your AI assistant about NYC 311

nyc-311-mcp connects an AI assistant like Claude to New York City's 311 data. Ask it how to report a problem, what your community district complains about most, how long agencies take to close requests, or whether alternate side parking is on today. It answers from the City's own data and shows you where each answer came from.

We built it at BetaNYC. It's free, it's open source, and it never files anything with 311 for you.

See it work

A neighbor asks about a pothole. The assistant finds the City's 311 article for it and counts how often people in the same district reported it.

Some things shouldn't go to 311 at all. When a description sounds dangerous, the first line says to call 911.

Before you rely on an answer

  • 311 counts are reports, not problems. They show who reports and how, and not only how many problems there are.
  • "Closed" means an agency closed the request. It doesn't mean the problem was fixed. Read the agency's resolution text.
  • The district numbers come from BoardStat, built every night from NYC Open Data. They aren't official City statistics. Each answer links to the BoardStat page, so you can check.
  • We left ZIP codes out on purpose. ZIP codes are mail routes, not City districts.

Tell us what you find

If an answer is wrong, confusing, or missing something, open an issue. GitHub issues are public, so leave out names, phone numbers, home addresses, and your API key. A street corner or a district is enough.
