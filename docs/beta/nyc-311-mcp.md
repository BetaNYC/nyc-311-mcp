# The nyc-311-mcp beta has ended

[Beta MCPs](index.md) › nyc-311-mcp

Version 1.2.0 of `@betanyc/nyc-311-mcp` was released on November 9, 2026, after 19 beta
builds. It has all 21 tools from the beta, and it's on npm.

- **Install it:** [install guide](../install.md).
- **See every tool:** [tools page](../tools.html).
- **Were you testing the beta?** You can remove the `nyc-311-beta` entry from your app. The
  install guide shows how.

Thank you to everyone who tried it and told us what broke.
